# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Improvisers, improv teachers, and troupe/jam hosts — from beginners to experienced
performers. They reach for the app in the moment: mid-rehearsal, warming up backstage, running
a workshop, or hosting a jam. Context is mobile-first and hands-busy: one-handed taps between
exercises, often offline in a black-box theatre or rehearsal room. The job is "give me the next
prompt / structure / randomizer instantly so I can keep the energy in the room," not "sit and
read." Sessions are short, repeated, and interruptible.

## Product Purpose

An all-in-one improv toolkit: 26 focused tools (archetype wheel, scene generator, 60+ warmups,
character builder, prompt cards, audience suggestions, format library, timer, metronome,
soundscapes, Harold/Deconstruction structure callers, and more). It exists to remove friction
from running improv — no more scribbled lists or googling games mid-session. Success = a host or
player opens a tool, gets exactly what they need in one or two taps, and puts the phone down.
Free, installable PWA, fully offline, bilingual (English / Polish by domain).

## Brand Personality

Playful, generous, confident — a witty stage-manager friend, not a corporate utility. Voice is
warm, direct, and encouraging (stop-slop copy: active voice, no filler, no em-dashes). The 🐍
snake mascot carries the personality. The interface should feel premium and tactile while staying
joyful: craft over cuteness.

## Anti-references

- **Generic "AI app" slop**: three identical feature cards, uppercase tracked eyebrows on every
  section, gradient text, glassmorphism-by-default, side-stripe accent borders, emoji as UI icons.
- **Childish/toy aesthetics**: rainbow-confetti, cartoon bounce/elastic motion, Comic-Sans energy.
- **Cold productivity SaaS**: gray-on-gray dashboards, dense data tables, navy-and-gradient hero.
- **Rainbow per-feature theming**: the old build gave every tool its own hue; now ONE fresh-green
  accent is used identically and features are distinguished by their custom icon, not color.

## Design Principles

- **Instant over instructional** — the next prompt is one tap away; never make a busy host read.
- **One confident accent** — a single fresh green throughout; color restraint reads as craft.
- **Distinct by icon, not color** — a bespoke rounded-line icon per tool gives identity without
  a rainbow.
- **Tactile, not bouncy** — fluid exponential ease-out and real press feedback; no cartoon
  overshoot.
- **Earns "a human made this"** — passes the AI-slop test: no eyebrows-everywhere, no identical
  card grids, no stock-icon tells.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large/UI glyphs ≥3:1 (green accent verified against ink/white).
Functional color (archetype palette, status levels, emotion families) always paired with icon or
text, never color-alone. Touch targets ≥44px with ≥8px gaps. Global `prefers-reduced-motion`
guard (animations collapse to instant). Visible `focus-visible` rings on all interactives.
Bilingual EN/PL; layouts tolerate longer Polish strings.
